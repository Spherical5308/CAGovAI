# 90-Day Rollout Plan

> A week-by-week plan for putting the framework into effect at [State Entity]. **Day 0** is the Policy's effective date, which also opens the 90-day Legacy Use registration window ([Procedure 02, section 4](../procedures/02-AI-Inventory.md#4-legacy-use-registration-first-90-days)).
>
> The plan is guidance, not a requirement of law or SAM/SIMM. Steps that carry out a requirement name it in the Source column. Adjust the timing to your entity's size; keep the Day 0 and Day 90 milestones, because the Policy ties them together.

## At a glance
| Phase | When | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Prepare | Weeks −6 to 0 | Tailor the documents, appoint the people, set up intake and storage, get the Director's approval |
| 2. Launch | Day 0 to Day 30 | Announce, open intake, hold the first Committee meeting, start registration, discovery and training |
| 3. Register and triage | Day 31 to Day 60 | Screen and tier Legacy Uses, approve Approved Uses for existing Tools, train specialist roles |
| 4. Close the window | Day 61 to Day 90 | Chase gaps, reconcile discovery against registrations, close the window |
| 5. Steady state | After Day 90 | Monthly Committee, quarterly inventory checks, Re-reviews, annual report |

## Measures to report
Track from Day 0 so the first annual report has a baseline:
- Requests received, decided and returned, by tier, with turnaround against the Charter §6 targets.
- Legacy Uses registered, and how many were found only by discovery.
- Training completion by audience.
- Open approval conditions, overdue Re-reviews, Exceptions and their expiry dates.
- Residual Risk Ratings of High accepted by the Director, and Risk Acceptances after a dissent.
- AI incidents and Suspensions.
