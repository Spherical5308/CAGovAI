# AI Governance Committee: Kickoff Meeting Agenda

> For the Committee's first meeting, in weeks 1 to 2 after the Policy's effective date ([Rollout Plan](./90-Day-Rollout-Plan.md) task 2.4). Allow 90 minutes. The AI Governance Coordinator prepares the pack and records every decision on a [Decision Record](../forms/Decision-Record.md). A standing agenda for later monthly meetings follows.

**Meeting:** AI Governance Committee, first meeting · **Date:** [date] · **Chair:** Executive Sponsor · **Secretary:** AI Governance Coordinator

## Pre-reads (sent at least 5 business days before, Charter §2.3)
1. [AI Governance Policy](../policy/AI-Governance-Policy.md) and [Committee Charter](../policy/AI-Governance-Committee-Charter.md), as signed.
2. [Procedure Map](../PROCEDURE-MAP.md), sections 1 to 4: the process, swimlane, routing rules and tier matrix.
3. [Rating Scales](../forms/Rating-Scales.md), including the risk tolerance table.
4. Draft [Approved Uses Register](../forms/Approved-Uses-Register.md) for each AI Tool already licensed.
5. Legacy Use registration status and the discovery sweep plan.
6. Training plan ([Training Outline](./GenAI-Training-Outline.md)).

## Agenda
| # | Item | Lead | Time | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Welcome and purpose.** Why the Committee exists (SAM 4986.9(A) multidisciplinary governance team) and what success looks like in the first year. | Chair | 5 min | — |
| 2 | **Membership and quorum.** Confirm Voting and Advisory Seat holders and written delegates. Quorum is 4 of 6 Voting Seats including the Chair or delegate. | Chair | 10 min | Membership list recorded |
| 3 | **How we decide.** Majority of Voting Seats present; ties fail; approve, approve with conditions, defer or reject; High items go to the Director as a recommendation. No veto: a recorded ISO dissent needs a Director-signed Risk Acceptance (ADR-0001). Attestation on SIMM 5305-F is not a vote (Charter §4.4). | Coordinator | 10 min | Understood; questions noted |
| 4 | **Conflicts of interest.** A seat whose own unit submitted a Request does not vote on it (Charter §4.2). Each member names any current interests. | Chair | 5 min | Interests recorded |
| 5 | **Risk tolerance.** Walk through the Rating Scales and the residual-risk table: the decider accepts up to Medium, only the Director accepts High (ADR-0005). Agree whether to recommend any change to the Director. | ISO, Privacy Officer | 10 min | Tolerance confirmed, or a change recommended |
| 6 | **The process end to end.** Intake, Prohibited-use screen, triage, Assessment Packages, CDT routing (Procedure 01 §4), turnaround targets (Charter §6). Confirm the Triage Panel designees and the monthly consent agenda. | Coordinator | 15 min | Triage Panel confirmed |
| 7 | **Decision: Approved Uses for existing Tools.** Vote on each draft Approved Uses Register. Each approved register makes the listed uses Pre-approved. | Tool owner, CIO | 15 min | One Decision Record per Tool |
| 8 | **Legacy Use registration.** Status so far, discovery sweep plan, the Day 60 and Day 90 milestones, and what happens at Day 90 (unregistered AI becomes a security incident). | Coordinator, CIO | 10 min | Owners and dates confirmed |
| 9 | **Training.** Audiences, courses and completion tracking (SAM 4986.13). | HR/Training lead | 5 min | Plan endorsed |
| 10 | **Calendar and actions.** Set the monthly meeting date. Confirm written votes between meetings when no Voting Seat objects (Charter §3). Read back actions with owners and due dates. | Chair, Coordinator | 5 min | Calendar set; action list |

## Decisions to record
- Membership, delegates and declared interests.
- One Decision Record for each Approved Uses Register voted on.
- Any recommendation to the Director on the risk tolerance.
- The meeting calendar.

|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quorum, conflicts of interest for today's items | Chair |
| 2 | Minutes and open actions from last meeting | Coordinator |

| 8 | Quarterly (every third meeting): AI Inventory review, overdue Re-reviews, open conditions, Risk Register entries | Coordinator, ISO |
