# AI Workforce Impact Assessment

> Required for High Requests, and for any Request that hits Trigger T7 (employees' terms, conditions or monitoring). Prepared by the Use Case owner with the Labor Relations Officer. EO N-12-23 directs the State to support its workforce through GenAI adoption; SIMM 5305-F requires the Labor Relations Officer to receive every GenAI risk assessment.
>
> Section structure is adapted from the aipolicytracker.org AI Workforce Impact Assessment template (CC BY 4.0); changes were made. See [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md).

> **How to complete this form**
> - **Who:** the Use Case owner drafts it. The Labor Relations Officer completes section 3 and signs.
> - **When:** as part of the Assessment Package. Start early: if notice to a union or meet and confer is needed, it can take longer than the rest of the package.
> - **Send to:** the AI Governance Coordinator, with the rest of the Assessment Package.
> - In section 1, use one row per classification affected. Give numbers of positions, not names of employees.
> - If you don't know an answer yet, write what you know and when you will know more. Do not leave it blank.

## 2. Effects on work
1. Workload: does the AI change volume, pace or productivity expectations?
2. Duties: are any duties added, removed or moved between classifications?
3. Monitoring and evaluation: will AI output, usage data or logs be used to monitor, evaluate or discipline employees? (If yes, Tier Floor is at least Moderate and Chief Counsel must review.)
4. Health, safety and accommodation: any effect on ergonomics, stress, or reasonable accommodations?
5. Positions: are any positions expected to be reduced or eliminated? If so, numbers, timeline and basis.
