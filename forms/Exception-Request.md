# AI Standard Exception Request

> Exceptions apply to a **Standard** only, never to the Policy, to law, or to SAM/SIMM requirements, and never to a Prohibited use. Maximum 12 months; one renewal. See [Procedure 05](../procedures/05-Exceptions.md).

> **How to complete this form**
> - **Who:** the Use Case owner.
> - **When:** as soon as you know a Standard's requirement cannot be met, and before go-live or before the requirement falls due. An Exception is not in force until the Director signs.
> - **Send to:** the AI Governance Coordinator, who routes it to the ISO and Privacy Officer for comment and then to the Committee.
> - Name the Standard and section exactly. Attach anything that shows the compensating controls are real (configuration, contract clause, procedure).
> - **Risk** in section 3 is rated with the [Rating Scales](./Rating-Scales.md), printed at the end of the Word version.
