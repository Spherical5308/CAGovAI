# Changelog

Notable changes to CAGovAI. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`: MAJOR changes the framework's shape (decision rights, tiers, scope); MINOR adds or substantially revises a document; PATCH corrects citations or wording.

## [Unreleased]
### Added
- Fillable Word versions of all seven forms and assessments (`forms/word/`), and an Excel AI Inventory workbook with dropdowns and a field guide (`inventory/AI-Inventory.xlsx`), built from the Markdown and CSV sources by `scripts/build_office.py`.
- Field guide for `pre-approved-log.csv` in the inventory README.
- Rating Scales (`forms/Rating-Scales.md`): definitions of Low / Medium / High Likelihood and Severity, a Risk Rating matrix and a documented AI risk tolerance saying who accepts residual risk (decider for the tier up to Medium; only the Director for High; security and privacy risks also go on the SIMM 5305-B Risk Register), aligned with NIST AI RMF GOVERN 1.3, 2.3, MAP 1.5 and SAM 5305.1/5305.6. Used by the AI Impact Assessment, Risk Acceptance and Exception Request.
- Decision record ADR-0005: only the Director accepts a High residual risk.
- Glossary terms Risk Rating, Residual risk and Risk tolerance; Policy §4.2 and the Authority Crosswalk point to the Rating Scales.
- CI job that rebuilds the diagrams, Word forms and Excel inventory and fails if the committed files are stale (`scripts/check_generated.py`). Builds are now reproducible.
- Procedure 01 Stage E and Procedure Map step 9a: how residual risk is accepted.
- "How to complete this form" instructions (who, when, where to send) at the top of every form.
### Changed
- AI Impact Assessment section 4 and Risk Acceptance section 2 gain a Risk Rating column. Decision Record records the residual Risk Rating and any Risk Register entry.
- README title now says what the repository is: "CAGovAI: AI Governance Framework for California State Entities".
- Procedure map diagrams (§1 overview, §2 swimlane, §6.2 Legacy Use registration): Mermaid replaced by standard lane-and-phase diagrams, each published as an editable draw.io file and an SVG, generated by `scripts/build_diagrams.py`.
- Procedure map §6.2: unregistered AI after 90 days is now shown as a security incident, matching Procedure 02 §4 (was "policy violation").

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-24
First public draft.
### Added
- Contributor and community files: CONTRIBUTING, Code of Conduct, SECURITY policy, issue and pull request templates, CODEOWNERS.
- Link-check workflow and script.
- Procedure map, glossary and four decision records (ISO votes without veto; one intake for all AI; approve Tools once; procurement as enforcement gate).
- AI Governance Policy and AI Governance Committee Charter.
- Standards: Acceptable Use; Data and Tier Floor; Autonomy and Agent.
- Procedures: Request and Approval; AI Inventory; Monitoring and Re-review; AI Incident Handling; Exceptions.
- Forms and assessments: Intake Form, Approved Uses Register, Decision Record, Risk Acceptance, Exception Request, AI Impact Assessment, Workforce Impact Assessment.
- AI Inventory CSV templates and field guide.
- Authority Crosswalk (SAM, SIMM, Government Code, Civil Code, NIST AI RMF).
- Non-Executive Branch adoption note.
- Licensing: CC BY 4.0 for documents, MIT for code.
### Changed
- CDT submission routed by tier and procurement route (CDT PS-033, June 30, 2026; SAM 4986.9 rev. 11/2025).
- Citations corrected against current text (GC 7929.210, 8592.45, 11546.45.5, 11549.66; SAM 5305.5; GC 12270–12279; Civil Code 3111).
