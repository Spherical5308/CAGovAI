SAM 4986.12 requires each Executive Branch State Entity's Acceptable Use Policy to explain (1) the risks of GenAI, (2) how to use it safely and (3) the specific SAM requirements. This Standard does that. Every user acknowledges it before first use and each year after.

## 1. The risks, in plain words
- **AI can be confidently wrong.** It invents facts, citations and figures ("hallucinations").
- **AI can be unfair.** It can repeat bias in the data it learned from.
- **AI can leak data.** What you type may be stored, reviewed by a vendor or used to train a model, unless the Tool is approved and configured not to.
- **AI can be manipulated.** Hidden instructions in documents, emails or web pages can redirect it ("prompt injection").
- **AI output is a record.** Prompts and outputs created for State business are public records.

## 2. Before you use AI
1. Use only a **Tool** listed as approved in the [AI Inventory](../inventory/), with your State-issued account, on State-issued or State-approved equipment.
2. Complete the GenAI training required for your role (SAM 4986.13).
3. Check that what you want to do is an **Approved Use** for that Tool. If it is not, or if any **Trigger** applies, submit a Request first (see the [Data and Tier Floor Standard](./Data-and-Tier-Floor-Standard.md#2-triggers)).

## 3. Rules for every use
1. **Data.** Do not enter confidential, sensitive or personal information (Civil Code 1798.3, 1798.29) into any AI Tool unless that Tool is approved for that data class.
2. **Verify.** Review and verify every output for accuracy, relevance and bias before you use it. Output used in any decision must be verified by a person qualified to judge it (SAM 4986.12).
3. **Own it.** You are responsible for anything you use, send or publish, whether or not AI drafted it. Do not present AI-generated content as entirely your own work.
4. **Disclose.** Label content created or substantially altered by GenAI when it goes to the public or is used in government services (GC 11549.66). Use: "This [text/image/audio/video] was generated, in whole or in part, using artificial intelligence." Include how to reach a person.
5. **Opt out of training.** Where a Tool offers to use your data to train its models, opt out (SAM 4986.12).
6. **Respect rights.** Do not infringe copyright or other intellectual property, and follow open-source licence terms.
7. **Accounts.** Do not use your State email or other State-identifying information to register for an AI Tool that is not approved.
8. **Ownership.** Outputs you create for [State Entity] belong to the State; you waive personal ownership of them (SAM 4986.12).
9. **Keep records.** Do not delete prompts, outputs or logs except as the retention schedule allows.
10. **Report.** Report any entry of confidential, sensitive or personal data into an unapproved Tool, and any harmful or suspicious output, to the Information Security Officer at [contact] right away.

## 4. Prohibited uses
AI must not be used to do, create or support any of the following. A Request for any of them is stopped at screening (Risk Tier: **Prohibited**).

| # | Prohibited use | Source |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Illicit content | SAM 4986.12 |
| P2 | Content that is politically regulated, unlawful, or lacks broad factual verification or consensus | SAM 4986.12 |
| P3 | Spoofing or fraud, including deepfakes, impersonation, misinformation, phishing or social engineering | SAM 4986.12; SIMM 5305-F Part 2 |
| P4 | Content that undermines the principles of diversity, equity, inclusion and accessibility of individuals | SAM 4986.12 |
| P5 | A decision that affects a person's rights, benefits, services, liberty or employment made by AI **without** human verification | SAM 4986.12 |
| P6 | Unlawful discrimination against a protected class | GC 11135 |
| P7 | Improper systematic, indiscriminate or large-scale monitoring, surveillance or tracking of individuals | SIMM 5305-F Part 2 |
| P8 | Social scoring: rating people's trustworthiness or worth from their behaviour or traits to treat them less favourably | [State Entity] policy |
| P9 | Real-time remote biometric identification of people in public spaces, or biometric categorisation inferring protected characteristics | [State Entity] policy |
| P10 | Inferring the emotions of employees or job applicants, except for a documented medical or safety reason | [State Entity] policy |
| P11 | Selling or sharing State data with data brokers | Civil Code 1798.99.80–.89; SIMM 5305-F Part 2 |

P8–P10 go beyond what SAM requires. They are recommended because they carry severe civil-rights risk; a State Entity adopting this Standard may adjust them with Chief Counsel's advice.
