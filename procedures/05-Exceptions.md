# Procedure 05: Exceptions

| | |
|---|---|
| **Authority** | [AI Governance Policy](../policy/AI-Governance-Policy.md) section 4.14 |
| **Owner** | AI Governance Coordinator |
| **Effective date** | [date] |
| **Map** | [PROCEDURE-MAP.md](../PROCEDURE-MAP.md) step 18 |

## 1. Purpose
Allow a time-limited departure from a **Standard** when meeting it is not yet practical, while keeping the risk visible and owned.

## 2. Rules
1. Exceptions apply to Standards only. There are no exceptions to the Policy, to State or federal law, or to SAM and SIMM requirements that [State Entity] does not own. (A departure from SAM or SIMM needs the relevant control agency's own exemption process.)
2. No Exception may permit a Prohibited use.
3. An Exception lasts at most 12 months. It may be renewed once, through a new Exception Request showing progress.
4. Each Exception has compensating controls, a named owner and a plan to reach compliance.

## 3. Steps
| Step | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use Case owner | Submit the [Exception Request](../forms/Exception-Request.md) to the Coordinator. |
| 2 | Coordinator | Confirm the request is for a Standard and is complete; get written input from the ISO and Privacy Officer. |
| 3 | Committee | Vote. |
| 4 | Director | Sign if approved. |
| 5 | Coordinator | Record the Exception and its expiry in the AI Inventory; notify the owner 60 days before expiry. |
| 6 | Use Case owner | At expiry: show compliance, request renewal, or stop the non-compliant activity. |
