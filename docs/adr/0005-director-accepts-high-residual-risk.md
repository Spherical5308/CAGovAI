# Only the Director accepts a High residual risk

Each risk found in assessment gets a Risk Rating (Low, Medium or High) from the [Rating Scales](../../forms/Rating-Scales.md), which also state [State Entity]'s AI risk tolerance. The Triage Panel or Committee accepts residual risk up to Medium as part of its decision, on the Director's behalf. A High residual Risk Rating is outside tolerance: the default is not to proceed until it is brought down, and only the Director can accept it, in writing, until the next Re-review. Security and privacy risks still at Medium or High also go on the ISO's SIMM 5305-B Risk Register.

We chose this to follow the sources rather than invent a scheme: SAM 5305.1 and SIMM 5305-B make the state entity head responsible for all risks, SAM 5305.6 and NIST AI RMF MAP 1.5 require a documented risk tolerance, NIST AI RMF GOVERN 2.3 puts AI risk decisions with executive leadership, and NIST AI RMF says development and deployment should cease where risk is unacceptable until it can be managed. It means a Moderate Request can reach the Director, but only when a High residual risk remains. This is separate from ADR-0001: a recorded ISO dissent still needs a Risk Acceptance whatever the rating.
