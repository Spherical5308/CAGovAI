# Adopting this framework outside the Executive Branch

For constitutional offices and other State Entities that are not under the Governor's direct authority (GC 11546.1(e)), such as entities headed by constitutional officers or independently established under GC 11000.

## Recommendation
**Adopt the whole framework as written, including sections marked *(EB)*.** Where an *(EB)* section relies on a SAM chapter that does not bind you, keep the control and change only the citation to your own authority (for example, "by policy of [State Entity]" instead of "SAM 4986.12").

Why: exemption is a grey area in practice. Most non-Executive Branch entities stay connected to CDT through procurement, projects, budget and statewide services, and many follow the Executive Branch process anyway. One process that already meets the Executive Branch bar avoids rework if CDT, the Department of Finance or a control agency later asks for it, and it is easier to defend in an audit.

## What already applies to every State Entity
SAM 4986.2 defines "State Entity" to include entities headed by constitutional officers and those described in GC 11000. Several GenAI chapters apply to **all** State Entities, not only the Executive Branch:

| SAM section | Applies to | Effect on this framework |
|---|---|---|
| 4986.1–4986.2 Introduction, definitions | All State Entities | — |
| 4986.3 Use identification and high-risk inventory | Executive Branch | Keep the AI Inventory anyway; see GC 11546.45.5 below |
| 4986.4 Training data and transparency | **All State Entities** | Applies as written |
| 4986.5 Hosting | Executive Branch | Keep; substitute your own hosting authority if not using CDT |
| 4986.6 POC and MVP | Executive Branch | Keep the sandbox and public-data rule as good practice |
| 4986.7–4986.8 IT project planning and management | Executive Branch | Follow if your project is under CDT oversight or delegation |
| 4986.9 GenAI procurement | **All State Entities** (except as exempted by statute or policy) | SIMM 5305-F, CDT submission and consultation rules, PTA/PIA, and the multidisciplinary governance team **apply** |
| 4986.10 Privacy | **All State Entities** | Disclaimer, human contact and opt-out apply |
| 4986.11 Security | **All State Entities** | Incident reporting, NIST AI RMF, risk register, 5305-F/5310-C per system, SAM 5335 monitoring apply |
| 4986.12 Acceptable use | Executive Branch | Keep the Acceptable Use Standard as your own policy |
| 4986.13 Workforce training | Executive Branch | Keep; training is still needed to operate safely |

Source: the Scope line in each SAM 4986 chapter (checked November 2026). Confirm against the current SAM before relying on it.

## Other ways CDT and control agencies stay involved
Ask Chief Counsel which of these apply to [State Entity]:
- **Procurement:** buying through statewide contracts or programs (e.g. DGS Software Licensing Program, CDT offerings) brings their terms, including GenAI disclosure language under SCM Vol. 2 Ch. 23. CDT PS-033's SITeS route is written for Executive Branch entities; others are encouraged to do their own risk assessments.
- **Projects:** IT projects that come under CDT's project approval or oversight follow the Project Delivery Lifecycle (SIMM 19H).
- **Budget:** budget change proposals with IT components are commonly reviewed with CDT and the Department of Finance.
- **Statewide services:** using CDT-hosted services, networks or security operations brings CDT's terms and incident reporting.
- **High-risk ADS inventory:** GC 11546.45.5 directs CDT to inventory systems used by "state agencies". Check that section's definition to see whether it reaches [State Entity]; CDT asks each agency to file, even with a nil return.

## Section-by-section guidance
| Framework section | If not bound by the SAM chapter |
|---|---|
| Policy §4.6 and Procedure 01 §4, CDT submission | SAM 4986.9 applies to all State Entities, so follow it. The SITeS "Low: do not submit" rule is Executive Branch only; if you do not buy through SITeS, submit as SAM 4986.9 requires unless CDT tells you otherwise. |
| Policy §4.8, hosting, POC, projects | Keep the controls. Replace "CDT-approved sandbox/data center" with your approved equivalent if you do not use CDT. |
| Procedure 02 §6, high-risk inventory to CDT | File if GC 11546.45.5 reaches you or CDT asks; otherwise keep the export ready. |
| Acceptable Use Standard | Adopt as your own policy; cite [State Entity] authority instead of SAM 4986.12–.13. |
| Committee Charter | No change. SAM 4986.9(A) requires a multidisciplinary governance team for all State Entities. |
| Director approval of High | Use your entity's equivalent: the constitutional officer or their designated chief deputy. "Parent Agency" escalation usually does not apply. |
