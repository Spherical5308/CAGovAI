## 1. Purpose
The AI Governance Committee is the multidisciplinary governance team that SAM 4986.9(A) requires. It decides whether [State Entity] uses AI where risk is Moderate or High, and continuously oversees security, privacy, legal, operational and workforce risk across the life of each approved Use Case.

### 2.3 Secretariat
The **AI Governance Coordinator** is secretary to the Committee. The Coordinator prepares agendas, circulates Assessment Packages at least 5 business days before a meeting, records decisions and votes, and updates the AI Inventory. The Coordinator does not vote.

### 2.4 Delegates
Each seat holder may name one standing delegate in writing to the Chair. A delegate votes with the seat's full authority. A seat holder may not delegate to a person who reports to the requesting business unit for that item.

## 3. Meetings and quorum
1. The Committee meets monthly. The Chair may call additional meetings, including on 2 business days' notice to confirm a Suspension.
2. **Quorum** is 4 of the 6 Voting Seats, including the Chair or the Chair's delegate.
3. Meetings may be held in person or virtually. Items may be decided by written vote between meetings if no Voting Seat objects to deciding it that way.

## 4. Decisions

### 4.1 How the Committee decides
1. Each Voting Seat has one vote. A motion passes with a majority of Voting Seats present and voting. On a tie, the motion fails and the item returns to the Coordinator with the Committee's questions.
2. The Committee may: **approve**, **approve with conditions** (each condition has an owner and a due date), **defer** (with specific questions), or **reject** (with reasons).
3. For **High** items, the Committee's decision is a recommendation to the Director.

### 4.2 Conflicts of interest
A seat holder whose own program or unit submitted the Request does not vote on it and is not counted toward quorum for that item. The Business/Program Representative's delegate, or another Deputy Director named by the Chair, votes instead. The requesting program presents its Request but does not vote.

### 4.3 Information Security Officer dissent
The ISO's vote counts the same as every other Voting Seat (ADR-0001). When the ISO votes against an item that otherwise passes:
1. The ISO's reasons are recorded in the minutes and in the Use Case's AI Inventory record.
2. The approval does not take effect until the Director signs a [Risk Acceptance](../forms/Risk-Acceptance.md) naming the residual risks the ISO identified.
3. The same applies to a dissent from the Privacy Officer or Chief Counsel on a matter within their legal duties.

### 4.4 Assessment Attestation is not a vote
The CIO's and ISO's signatures on SIMM 5305-F or SIMM 150 attest that the assessment is complete and that its risks are accurately stated. Signing does not approve the Request, and refusing to sign is appropriate only where the assessment is incomplete or inaccurate, not because the signer disagrees with the Use Case.

## 5. Standing responsibilities
1. Decide Moderate Requests and recommend High Requests.
2. Receive the Triage Panel's Low decisions as a monthly consent agenda; any Voting Seat may pull an item for full discussion.
3. Hear appeals of Prohibited determinations.
4. Confirm, lift or convert to Re-review any **Suspension** within 10 business days.
5. Approve **Exceptions** for the Director's signature.
6. Approve and change each approved Tool's **Approved Uses**.
7. Review the AI Inventory quarterly, including overdue Re-reviews, open conditions and expiring Exceptions.
8. Report annually to the Director on AI use, risk, incidents and turnaround against targets.
9. Recommend changes to the Policy and Standards.

## 6. Turnaround targets
Measured in business days from a complete Assessment Package, excluding time waiting on CDT:

| Tier | Target |
|---|---|
| Pre-approved | Immediate |
| Low | 10 |
| Moderate | 30 |
| High | 60 |

The Coordinator reports performance against these targets to the Committee each quarter.

## 7. Records
Agendas, Assessment Packages, minutes, votes, Risk Acceptances and decisions are records kept under [State Entity]'s retention schedule. Completed SIMM 5305-F, SIMM 150 and security reviews are confidential (GC 7929.210) and are kept in a restricted location.
